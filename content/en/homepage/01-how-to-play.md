Between August 2018 and June 2019, Magnus Carlsen went **112 games unbeaten** — a mix of classical, rapid, and blitz — against the world's best players. Play through every game in that streak, find his moves, and score against par.

Each game starts a few moves in. You take Magnus's side and type the move you think he played, one move at a time. After each of your moves, his opponent's reply appears on the scoresheet.

Every move is worth **3 points** (par 3). That share is each move's **par**:

- Right on the first try earns the full par (3 points).
- Each wrong guess takes one point off.
- **Hint** shows the board for that move. It doesn't use up a try, but one point comes off the move's score when you find it.
- When the tries run out, the move is shown and you score 0 for it. **Show me** does the same at once.
- A move that isn't legal in the position costs nothing.

Try to play with the board hidden and keep the position in your head. If that is too hard at first, switch on "Show the board as I play".

The games are grouped by opening in the menu below.
