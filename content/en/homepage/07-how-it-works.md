Each game is one page made from its moves. Every move is worth **3 points** (par 3), shared evenly, so a 30-move game is worth 90 points total. The page checks every move you type with a chess move generator, so it knows which moves are legal and which one was played in the game. Games are sorted into the opening menus by their first moves.

The 112 games come from Magnus Carlsen's historic unbeaten streak from August 2018 through June 2019, spanning classical, rapid, and blitz games against the world's top players — including the 2018 World Championship match against Fabiano Caruana and back-to-back world rapid and blitz titles.

## Tools
- [chess.js](https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js) checks the moves you type
- [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) with the [hugo-scroll](https://github.com/zjedi/hugo-scroll) theme builds the site
- Games compiled from Magnus Carlsen's complete game collection (2000–2026)
